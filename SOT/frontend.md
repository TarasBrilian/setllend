# Frontend: pages, look, and data

> **Status: APPROVED v0.2 (2026-11-03), D47 and D48.** The UI is built first against mock data, before the backend API exists. Nothing in it sends a transaction yet. The collateral model is D48, which the contract implements since contract-interface v0.6 (2026-11-03, [contract-interface.md](contract-interface.md#math)); the UI still shows it on mock data.

The frontend is `frontend/` (Next.js, `wagmi`, `viem`, see [tech-stack.md](tech-stack.md)). It reads only the backend API ([backend.md](backend.md#api)), except wallet balances, which it reads from chain, and signs intents with the user's EVM or NEAR wallet (D51). This file owns what the user sees.

## Look

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Theme | Dark only. Background warm black `#0C0A09`, accent orange `#F97316`. Surfaces and borders from the warm-grey (`stone`) scale |
| Type | Figtree (`next/font/google`). Numbers use tabular figures |
| Logo | Orange SVG mark plus the word "Setllend" |
| Target | Desktop first. Mobile layout is deferred |
| Number fields | Amounts and rates accept only digits and one decimal point. A comma is read as the decimal point, and any other keystroke or paste is ignored. Prefilled values (Max, balances) are plain decimals, never exponent notation such as `1e-7` |

## Intro

A logo animation over the Market page, about 4.8 s, that ends as the header logo.

- **When:** only when the first page load of a browser tab is `/`. It does not replay on reload, on later visits to `/` in the same tab, or when the tab first opened another page. It never shows under the OS "reduce motion" setting. The dev server (`npm run dev`) replays it on every load of `/`, to make it easy to review.
- **Skip:** a "Skip" button in the bottom-right corner, shown from 0.3 s until the pills start flying. It fades the intro out in 0.25 s and shows the page as it is after the intro.
- **Sequence:** on the site's own background, which covers the header and page, the top (orange) pill of the mark slides in from the right edge and the bottom (light orange) pill from the left a moment later, meeting as the mark in the center. The mark moves left while "Setllend" slides out from behind it, keeping the pair centered. The letters then spread apart, "S" staying in place, and fade one by one, center first. The two pills then fly to the header logo in the top-left corner, shrinking to its size on smooth curves: the top pill rises and lands from above, the bottom pill a moment later dips and rises to land from below, while the background fades so the Market page shows behind them. They join as the header mark, which gives a small bounce. The header's "Setllend" slides out from behind it, then the "Demo data" badge fades in.
- **Build:** CSS (`globals.css`) and the Web Animations API, no animation library. A small inline script in `<head>` turns it on before first paint, marks the tab session in `sessionStorage`, and animates the pills' flight, measuring the header logo's position once the mark and word have settled, about 1 s before the flight.

## Pages

### Header (every page)

- Logo on the left. Navigation in the middle: **Market** (`/`) and **Portfolio** (`/portfolio`).
- No Deposit button in the header: deposits start from Portfolio's My Assets, and order forms can also deposit inline through 1Click.
- **Connect** on the right opens one menu with two parts (D51): EVM wallets found through EIP-6963 (D14), and NEAR wallets through NEAR Wallet Selector (Meteor, MyNearWallet, Intear). Once connected it shows the account and its network: the short address and the chain (Base or Arbitrum) for EVM, the account ID for NEAR. Its menu: copy the account, switch chain (EVM), switch between a connected EVM and NEAR account, disconnect. The last account connected is the active one.

### Market (`/`)

- **Hero:** "Welcome to Setllend" and a tagline, no user name (an EVM wallet has none). Two figures: before connecting, protocol totals (total supplied = note value outstanding plus open `lend` orders, active loans = total debt face); after connecting, the user's own (supplied, borrowed). An abstract orange gradient shape on the right, drawn in SVG.
- **One card per open maturity** (2026-11-13, 11-27, 12-25, plus any demo maturity, see [risk-params.md](risk-params.md)). Each card shows USDC, the maturity date and days left, and:

  | Field | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | Borrow rate | Lowest `lend` order rate in the book: the APR a borrower pays to fill now |
  | Lend rate | Highest `borrow` / `sell_note` order rate: the APR a lender earns to fill now |
  | Max LTV | "Up to" the highest max LTV among active collateral tokens, with the icons of the accepted tokens (D48) |
  | Liquidity | USDC in open `lend` orders: what can be borrowed now |

  A side with no orders shows "–".
- **Borrow** and **Start Earning** open a modal on the same page:
  - Borrow: USDC amount, max APR (prefilled with the borrow rate), and collateral, from the deposited balance by default when it holds a collateral token (D66). At a match the USDC arrives in the Intents balance, and the user withdraws it from Portfolio to a chain or the NEAR account (D66). Collateral is a list of tokens with an amount each (one row to start, more through "Add token"), taken from the deposited balance or deposited now: from Arbitrum or Base, BTC from Bitcoin, and NEAR from the NEAR account or a 1Click swap (D52). With a loan already open in that maturity, a third source, "Existing loan", borrows more against the collateral the loan holds without adding any (D50). Preview: debt face at maturity, borrow limit (`Σ value × max_ltv`), health factor, and the price per token at which the loan becomes liquidatable, the other tokens held constant.
  - Start Earning: USDC amount, min APR (prefilled with the lend rate), and where the USDC comes from: the Intents balance, the default when it covers the amount, or a new deposit from a chain (D66). Preview: value at maturity and interest.
  - Submit shows the steps as a stepper: a deposit when one is needed, sign the order, order placed ([user-actions.md](user-actions.md)), then the result ([Data](#data), D66).
- **View market** links to the market detail page.

### Market detail (`/market/[maturity]`)

- **Top:** back link, USDC, a tab per open maturity (switching reloads the whole page for that maturity), and days left. On the right: this maturity's total supplied, active loans, and the oracle ETH price with its age.
- **Panels:** the chart, book, and order panels share one height of 30rem; a taller order form scrolls inside its panel with the submit button kept in view.
- **Chart panel**, two tabs, both `lightweight-charts` with the TradingView attribution logo on ([pitfalls.md](pitfalls.md#off-chain)):
  - Rate History: fill rates over time, ranges 1D, 7D, All, with a date and rate tooltip on hover.
  - Depth: cumulative USDC of lend offers and borrow requests by rate.
  - Both update in place as fills and books arrive, so a hover tooltip survives. Rate History plots one point per period (15 minutes on 1D, 1 hour on 7D, 3 hours on All): the principal-weighted average rate of its fills, since fills on both sides of the spread land close together and single fills would zigzag by the spread's width. Its rate axis spans at least 0.5 percentage points, so a few bps don't fill the chart.
- **Book panel**, two tabs:
  - Order Book: lend offers (bids) on top, highest rate first, then a row with the mid rate and the spread in bps, then borrow requests (asks, `sell_note` merged in) with the highest rate first. Each level shows its rate, USDC, and a size bar. A header row has APR, a color legend (Lend orange, Borrow green), and USDC; there are no side labels. Each side gets half of the panel's height, so the mid row stays in the middle, and shows as many levels as fit there, best first, hiding the ones farthest from the spread; the book never scrolls. Size bars scale to the largest level shown. The best level of each side, next to the spread, has a faint tint of its side's color. Clicking a lend offer opens the Borrow form at that rate in Limit mode; clicking a borrow request opens Lend.
  - **Live flashes (D56):** the book follows `/ws` pushes ([Data](#data)), and a changed row lights up for its whole width, then fades out over 350 ms. A fill flashes its maker's level bright in the side's color (orange for lend offers, green for borrow requests); a new order or a larger level flashes faint in the side's color; a cancel (a level that shrank or left without a trade) flashes faint gray. A level that empties stays at size 0 until its flash ends, then leaves. A sweep across levels lights each of them. The mid and spread row flashes gray when either changes. Flashes are worked out only from the pushed book and trades, compared with the previous ones: a new trade marks its maker's level (the side holding the trade's order at the trade's rate) as a fill, and a trade that arrives after its book turns that level's cancel flash into a fill.
  - Recent Trades: time, rate, USDC, and kind (`borrow` or `sell_note`). Trades that arrive while the tab is open flash once in orange.
- **Order panel**, tabs Lend, Borrow, Sell note (Sell note only with notes in this maturity), each in Limit or Market mode:
  - Limit: the user sets the rate. Any rest waits in the book.
  - Market: a UI shortcut, not a contract feature. The order is a limit order at the rate of the last book level needed to fill the amount. Each fill happens at the maker's rate (D17), so the form shows the average rate, and any amount beyond the book waits at that last rate.
  - Borrow uses the same multi-token collateral list as the modal, with a health factor bar, `Σ value × liq_threshold / debt face`. A debt face above the borrow limit blocks submit.
  - Sell note: note value to sell and the max rate. Preview: USDC received now.
- **Tables** for this maturity only, after connecting (before that, a Connect prompt): Open Orders (with cancel), Positions (the loan with repay, add collateral, and withdraw collateral; notes with sell or redeem), History (the user's events with time and status).

### Portfolio (`/portfolio`)

Before connecting: one card with a Connect prompt. After connecting, top to bottom:

- **My Position:** net value (notes + collateral at oracle price + available balances − debt face), interest to receive at maturity, average lend and borrow rates, and the lowest health factor across loans (health is per loan, never per account). A donut splits lending (notes), borrowed (debt face), collateral, and available (deposits in the Intents balance plus free balances in Setllend), with a legend that carries the values. "See all transactions" scrolls to History.
- **Maturity timeline:** per maturity, what the user receives from notes and what they owe, as paired bars.
- **All My Positions**, tabs:
  - Borrow: one row per loan (maturity). Maturity and days left ("Due" after maturity, liquidatable after the grace period), each collateral token with its amount and USD value, debt face and average rate, health factor, and the liquidation price per token. Actions: Repay, Add collateral, Withdraw collateral (one token at a time).
  - Lend: maturity and status ("Redeemable" after maturity), value at maturity and the part listed for sale, average rate and interest, and an estimate of what the notes would sell for now at the book's mid rate. Actions: Sell, Redeem.
  - Open Orders, every maturity, with cancel.
- **My Assets**, with a **Deposit** button. From Arbitrum or Base: USDC, ETH, or NEAR bought with a 1Click swap from USDC or ETH, then the 1Click steps. From Bitcoin: BTC through 1Click, sent from any Bitcoin wallet to the deposit address (about 15 minutes, D52). From NEAR (D51): NEAR or native USDC in one NEAR transaction, no 1Click; it asks for a NEAR wallet if none is connected (connecting one there only signs the deposit and keeps the active account), and credits the active account:
  - Wallet: for an EVM account, USDC and ETH on Base and Arbitrum, read from chain through `wagmi`; for a NEAR account, NEAR and USDC in the NEAR account, read from NEAR RPC.
  - Deposited: the Intents balance per token, "Not collateral" by default, with Add to collateral (pick a loan and an amount, preview the health factor before and after) and Withdraw.
  - Free balances in Setllend (failed payouts, redemptions), with Withdraw.
- **History:** every maturity, with a filter by action (Orders, Fills, Repay & redeem, Transfers for deposits, withdrawals, and collateral moves, Liquidations), and "Load more" for older pages (D65).

Every action opens a modal on the page: amount, a preview (for example the health factor after a collateral withdrawal), then the steps and their result (D66, simulated in mock mode). For a NEAR account whose key isn't registered yet, the first action that signs an intent adds a "Register your key on NEAR Intents" step (`add_public_key`, once). Repay, like Start Earning, pays from the Intents balance or a new deposit (D66). Chain pickers for payouts, withdrawals, and USDC payments also offer NEAR to a NEAR account, and default to it (D51). A NEAR account has no address on Arbitrum or Base, so any 1Click leg there (a payout, a withdrawal, or a deposit and its refund) asks for one, checked as a `0x` address. A Bitcoin leg (a BTC deposit's refund, or BTC sent out) asks every account for a Bitcoin address. ETH and BTC leave through 1Click to their own chain (D61); USDC and NEAR leave through 1Click to Arbitrum or Base, or to the NEAR account through an `ft_withdraw` or `native_withdraw` intent, signed alone (D64). Before a USDC withdrawal to the NEAR account, the UI reads `storage_balance_of` on the USDC contract from NEAR RPC, and without storage adds a "Register on USDC" step: a `storage_deposit` from the NEAR wallet, 0.00125 NEAR, once (D64). An implicit account that doesn't exist on chain yet can't send that transaction; the step then says to withdraw some NEAR first, which creates the account. Repay takes a partial amount or Max and notes that, before maturity, buying notes through a lend order in the same maturity is cheaper because they net against the debt (D20).

Add collateral and multi-token loans are D48. The contract has them (v0.6); the UI shows them on mock data until the backend serves them. Collateral tokens, their risk parameters, and frozen tokens come from one list ([risk-params.md](risk-params.md#per-token-parameters-d48)); a frozen token shows as such, can't be picked for a new borrow or a top-up, and gives no borrowing power in the loan math (D53). Add collateral offers only loans with debt that are not past maturity plus grace, as the contract requires (D53).

## Data

- Components read data only through `src/lib/api/`, whose functions mirror the REST endpoints in [backend.md](backend.md#api) and return the shared view types (`src/shared/`). Amounts stay decimal strings until display.
- **Mode (D65):** `src/lib/api/` serves either the HTTP client or the mocks, with the same functions. The UI reads `GET /health` at load and once a minute: `contract: "ready"` means the real API; any other answer (`checking`, `not_deployed`, `loading`) means the mocks, the "Demo data" badge, and simulated actions that say so. A backend that can't be reached (no answer within 5 s, or an HTTP error) means the mocks at load; once the UI is live, only three missed checks in a row switch it back, so one slow answer doesn't replace a user's real positions with mock ones (D65). A change of mode resets the cached API queries (wallet balances stay), so each one refetches from the new source; until the first answer every API call waits, so nothing is read from the wrong source first. The "Demo data" badge shows only in mock mode; it is always rendered and hidden otherwise, because the intro ends on its animation ([Intro](#intro)). The API URL is `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL`, by default `https://setllend-production.up.railway.app` ([backend.md](backend.md#deployment-railway-d28)); the WebSocket is the same host's `/ws` (`wss://`).
- **Reading the real API (D65):** `getMaturities` and `getMaturityStats` both come from `/api/maturities`, which carries the totals; `getAccountFills` is the `fill_totals` of `/api/accounts/:account`; prices and collateral tokens keep the fields the UI uses, and the extra ones (`price`, `conf`, `expo`, `feed_id`) come along. USDC on Base and Arbitrum, which the oracle doesn't list, shows at the NEAR USDC price, so wallet balances there get a value. History rows are mapped from the raw events: `order_placed` → placed, `order_filled` → filled (the side and kind from its `account_orders`), `order_cancelled` → cancelled (kind, maturity, and rate from its `order`), `repaid`, `redeemed`, `liquidated` (the borrower's only), `collateral_added`, `collateral_withdrawn`, `withdrawn`, and `payout_failed`; 1Click rows → deposit or withdrawal with their status; withdrawals to the NEAR account → withdrawal (D64). A `sell_note` row's amount is note shares, shown as USDC: one share is worth one USDC at maturity, give or take rounding, until bad debt is realized in that maturity ([contract-interface.md](contract-interface.md), D26). Events that name no single amount of the account (`netted`, `bad_debt_realized`) are left out. The three lists are merged by time, newest first (rows of one block in reverse event order), one page of 100 each, and "Load more" fetches the next page of every list that has one. A list with more pages may still hold rows newer than the oldest ones already loaded from another list, so rows older than the newest last-loaded row of such a list wait for its next page. If the first page fails, History says so and offers to try again.
- **Live updates on the real API (D65):** one WebSocket per tab, subscribed to the `book:` and `trades:` channels of the page and to `account:<id>` while connected. Book and trade pushes go into the query cache as with the mock (D56). An `event`, `intent`, or `oneclick` push for the account refetches the account, its history, and its lists. After a reconnect, every subscriber refetches what it shows, since pushes sent meanwhile are lost. The socket reconnects with a growing delay (1 s, doubling, at most 30 s) and subscribes again. The server has no unsubscribe and takes at most 50 topics per socket, so a socket that would pass that reconnects with the topics still wanted, and one nobody listens to closes after 5 s, so moving between pages doesn't reconnect.
- **Live updates (D56):** `subscribe(channels, onMessage)` in `src/lib/api/` mirrors WebSocket `/ws` ([backend.md](backend.md#websocket-ws)) for `book:<maturity>` and `trades:<maturity>`: the full book after every change, and every fill. `useLiveMarket` (`src/lib/use-live-market.ts`) writes the pushes into the query cache: a book replaces the cached `["book", maturity]`, a trade joins the cached `["trades", maturity]` (newest first, 200 kept). The Market grid subscribes to every open maturity, the market detail page to its own.
- **Market simulator (mock only, D56):** while anyone subscribes to a `book:` or `trades:` channel, the mock makes one random event every 0.5 to 1.5 s on each open maturity, each on its own timer: a fill at the best level, sweeping 2 levels 15% of the time, at each maker's rate (D17); a new order that never crosses the other side, either added to a level or as a new level on the 5 bps grid, more likely near the spread; or a cancel of all or part of a level. Amounts stay whole hundreds of USDC. Each side stays between 4 and 9 levels, and a side refilled at 4 gets its new level beyond its last one. A spread wider than 30 bps makes the next event a new level inside it. The mid drifts back to the seed book's mid, where the trade history ends: the further it strays, the likelier the events that move it back. It pushes the trades first, then the book, as `/ws` would. It exists so the book's flashes can be seen before real orders arrive, and leaves with the mocks.
- **D49 data in the backend API** since backend step 2 ([backend.md](backend.md#api)): protocol totals for the hero (`/api/stats`), per-maturity totals (`/api/maturities`), rate history (`/api/trades` with `since`), the user's event history (`/api/accounts/:account/history`, raw events with a `next` cursor, which the UI maps to its rows), the user's Intents balance (`deposits`), the collateral token list (`/api/collateral-tokens`), and fill totals per maturity (`fill_totals`). The UI's types in `src/lib/api/types.ts` follow these shapes (D65). 1Click deposits and withdrawals come from `/api/accounts/:account/oneclick` (D61) and withdrawals to the NEAR account from `/api/accounts/:account/withdrawals` (D64), merged into History by time; a withdrawal whose outcome is `sent` or `pending` shows as Unknown, with a hint to check the Intents balance and the NEAR account.
- **Actions (D66):** in live mode an action builds its intents with `shared/intents.ts`, with a nonce from `GET /api/intents/salt` and a deadline 10 minutes ahead, and the wallet signs them: an EVM account the payload string with `personal_sign` (ERC-191), a NEAR account through `signMessage` (NEP-413) in Meteor, Intear, or MyNearWallet, all in a popup or extension, with the 32 nonce bytes as a `Buffer` from the `buffer` package, since NEAR Wallet Selector refuses any other nonce ([pitfalls.md](pitfalls.md#off-chain)) (D68; `globalThis.Buffer` comes from the `buffer` package, which MyNearWallet needs). Before sending, a NEAR account checks `has_public_key` on `intents.near` for the key that signed (true for an implicit account's own key ✅ 2026-11-05); without it, the step "Register your key on NEAR Intents" sends one `add_public_key` transaction (1 yoctoNEAR, 30 Tgas). The signed payload goes to `POST /api/intents`, and the modal branches on `error.code`, not on `ok` alone: no error means done; `pending` (202) keeps the modal open until the `{type: "intent"}` push on `account:<id>`, which the user may stop waiting for; if that push is lost to a reconnect, or hasn't come after 11 minutes (the backend follows for 10), the modal says the result didn't reach the page and points to History and the transaction; `rejected`, `simulation_failed`, and the other codes show their `reason` with Try again; `payout_failed` means the action happened but the funds wait in the free balance ([backend.md](backend.md#submitting-user-intents)). The steps show their real state (waiting for the wallet, sent, done), and the result card says what happened, with the transaction on nearblocks (`https://nearblocks.io/txns/<hash>`). Afterwards the account, its fills, and its history refetch; the steps and result keep the action as submitted, even when the form behind them changes (a balance used up by the action). Amounts go into intents without float: typed digits past a token's decimals are dropped, and Max sends the exact raw balance, so an action never asks for more than there is. A redemption's result shows what the `redeemed` event paid, since the pool pays up to what it holds. Legs that deposit from another chain or exit to one follow the steps of D67 below. Mock mode keeps the simulated steps and its "Demo: nothing is signed or sent" note.
- **Deposits and exits (D61, D64, D67):** in live mode a modal runs its steps one after another and shows each one's state; a failed step is retried from that step, so a deposit that arrived is never sent again. Where the outcome can't be known (no answer from the relayer or to a 1Click submit, an unclear NEAR wallet error), the modal ends as unknown, marks the step with "?", and points to History instead of offering Try again, so nothing is sent twice (D67 point 15). Closing the modal stops the waiting, not what was sent.
  - **1Click deposit:** a stored quote (`POST /api/oneclick/quote`), `EXACT_OUTPUT` for the amount an order or a repayment still lacks after the Intents balance, `EXACT_INPUT` for a plain deposit. An EVM account's wallet then switches to the source chain and sends `amount_in` (ETH, or a USDC `transfer`) to `deposit_address`, the UI waits for its receipt (a reverted transfer fails the step) and forwards the hash to `deposit-tx`; retried after a wallet rejection, the same quote is sent again. A NEAR account on Arbitrum or Base sends from any wallet: the step shows the address and the exact amount, every digit of `amount_in`, since a rounded amount would arrive short. A Bitcoin deposit shows the address, the exact amount, a QR code, and a copy button; the refund address is required, and the modal can be closed while the BTC is on its way (about 15 minutes). The step waits for `SUCCESS` through the `oneclick` push and, in case a push is missed, `GET /api/oneclick/:id` every 10 s; `REFUNDED`, `FAILED`, or an expired quote fails it with the refund details, and Try again gets a new quote. Links: the source chain's transaction (Arbiscan, Basescan, mempool.space) and the NEAR one on nearblocks.
  - **Deposit from NEAR:** one transaction, so one wallet approval, in any of the three wallets (D68). NEAR goes to `wrap.near` with up to three actions: `storage_deposit` only when `storage_balance_of` is `null`, `near_deposit`, and `ft_transfer_call` to `intents.near` with `msg` = the account; USDC is one `ft_transfer_call` on the USDC contract.
  - **Preview:** in live mode, while the user types, a dry quote about 0.5 s after the last keystroke shows what is sent, what arrives, the time, and, when the same asset arrives, 1Click's fee (for `EXACT_OUTPUT`, `min_amount_in` minus what arrives, leaving out the slippage buffer 1Click refunds). Slippage is under Advanced, prefilled with the route's default (50 bps, 100 bps for swaps with NEAR) and sent as `slippage_bps`. A Borrow previews each collateral deposit at its route's default slippage. No preview is asked for before a needed refund address is entered.
  - **1Click withdrawal:** a stored quote whose payload the wallet signs once (ERC-191, or NEP-413 with `message`, `nonce`, and `recipient` as the backend returned them; a NEAR key not yet registered is registered first, as for any intent), then `POST /api/oneclick/:id/submit`. The step follows the status to `SUCCESS`, `REFUNDED`, or `FAILED`, with the destination transaction when 1Click reports it; Try again after a refund gets a new quote, and an unsubmitted quote is read again first in case 1Click took it.
  - **Withdrawal to the NEAR account (D64):** USDC first checks `storage_balance_of` on the USDC contract and, without storage, adds "Register on USDC" (a `storage_deposit` of 0.00125 NEAR from the wallet); then the `ft_withdraw` or `native_withdraw` intent goes through the relayer like any action (D66). NEAR always leaves as native NEAR.
  - **Chained:** "New deposit" in Lend, Repay, and Borrow deposits first, then signs the order. A borrow with new collateral from several chains deposits each token in turn; with BTC among them, the form suggests depositing BTC first and borrowing from Deposited. Withdraw collateral, Redeem, and moving a free balance to a chain sign the Setllend action, then withdraw what its event reports (a redemption can pay less than the notes' value); a `payout_failed` stops before the exit and points to the free balance in Portfolio. Borrow still withdraws from Portfolio (D66).
- Rate math (debt face, LTV, liquidation price) follows [contract-interface.md](contract-interface.md): simple interest on a 365-day year (D16), LTV on debt face.
- **Health factor (D54):** the API's `health_factor` uses conservative prices (`price − conf` for collateral, `price + conf` for debt), like the keeper ([backend.md](backend.md#api)). With the API's raw prices (live mode), the previews in `src/lib/collateral.ts` (health after a withdrawal or top-up, the borrow form's bar) take health from `healthFactor` in `shared/math.ts` with the prices of `/api/prices`, so a preview and the loan's own health agree (✅ 2026-11-05 against the backend, to all 9 decimals); the borrow limit, the most a withdrawal can take, and the liquidation price use the same conservative prices, the liquidation price shown as the mid price the oracle would report. USD values stay at mid prices. The mocks have no raw prices, so their previews are mid-price estimates.
